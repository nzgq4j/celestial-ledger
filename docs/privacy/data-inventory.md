# Data inventory and retention

Planned sensitive data includes account email, birth date/time, birthplace label and coordinates, timezone, chart evidence, selected recovery themes, order references, and generated reports.

Birth data and report content must not enter URLs, analytics, logs, tracing payloads, or public metadata. Completed reports and associated birth profiles expire one year after completion and may be deleted earlier. Payment records retain only fields required for reconciliation and legal obligations. Account deletion removes user-controlled content and records the minimum non-content audit outcome.
