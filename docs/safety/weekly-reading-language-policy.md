# Weekly astrological reading safety policy

Weekly readings are symbolic reflection, not scientifically validated prediction or professional advice. Output preserves agency, describes emphasis rather than certainty, and remains linked to calculated evidence.

It must not diagnose, prescribe, guarantee outcomes, predict crisis or harm, infer another person's thoughts or conduct, or provide medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or medication advice. It must not use fear, shame, inevitability, coercion, dependency, or costly remedy claims. Practical facts must be verified independently.

Unknown birth time excludes houses, angles, and exact-timing claims throughout the week. Unsupported days stay quiet rather than receiving filler. Release requires adversarial evaluation for determinism, professional-advice substitution, third-party mind-reading, crisis language, evidence fabrication, unknown-time violations, and translation drift.
