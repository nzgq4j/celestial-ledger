## Deferred product backlog additions

- `DR-D033` — **User account levels and capability entitlements**: `DEFER`. Design server-authoritative product levels, grants, usage allowances, lifecycle states, account/admin presentation, migration from existing users, and interaction with current report entitlements. Keep product levels separate from administrative RBAC. Acceptance requires deterministic entitlement evaluation, concurrency-safe usage, RLS/ownership tests, and approval of all commerce gates.
- `DR-D034` — **Evidence-linked data visualizations**: `DEFER`. Design renderer-neutral visualization data derived only from Stage A evidence for transit timelines, natal overlays, lunar sequences, theme convergence, life domains, and opportunity/risk timing. Acceptance requires evidence-to-mark traceability, unknown-time exclusions, accessible text equivalents, localization, print, visual regression, and performance tests.

Detailed requirements and sequencing are in [`BACKLOG.md`](BACKLOG.md).

## Replacement, deletion, and unresolved status

- No component is approved for replacement or deletion.
- `REQUIRES VALIDATION`: whether the saved birth-profile time zone is sufficient as the default daily observation zone, or whether a separate account time-zone preference is necessary.
- `REQUIRES VALIDATION`: daily retention may be shorter than one year, but it must never exceed the private-report maximum without a new privacy decision.
- `REQUIRES VALIDATION`: whether daily narrative generation uses the existing report model setting or a new `ai.models.dailyReading` setting.
