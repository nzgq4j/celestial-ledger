# Daily reading interpretation rules

## Purpose

Rules convert immutable celestial evidence into deterministic interpretive signals before any narrative model is called. They express the internal vocabulary of astrology directly while preventing the narrative layer from inventing calculations, timing, events, or certainty.

## Rule contract

Each rule contains:

```text
rule ID and semantic version
supported evidence kinds
required and forbidden conditions
birth-time requirement
aspect/orb/motion constraints
theme and life-domain tokens
temporal-state mapping
duration class
base relevance and intensity
supporting and moderating factors
contradiction tags
practical-application tokens
risk/watch tokens
reflective-question tokens
technical explanation token
editorial status and review date
```

Rules are repository-versioned and runtime-validated for the first release. A database editorial system is deferred until versioning, approvals, rollback, and consumer migration provide a measurable benefit.

## Evidence precedence

1. Calculated facts establish what is present and when.
2. Reviewed rules establish available meanings and applications.
3. Deterministic synthesis establishes priority, convergence, contradiction, and time horizon.
4. Approved user context changes emphasis and ordering only.
5. Narrative generation expresses the validated analysis in the selected language.

No later level may alter an earlier level.

## Initial rule families

- Transiting planet to natal planet by major aspect.
- Transiting planet to natal Ascendant/Midheaven when exact birth time is known.
- Transiting planet moving through a natal equal house when exact birth time is known.
- Current interplanetary major aspects that materially moderate personal transits.
- Moon sign and natal-house passage.
- Lunar phase and phase transition.
- Sign ingress, station, and repeated-pass state.
- Convergence of multiple signals on a life domain or theme.
- Separating factors, ending windows, and integrating phases.
- Applying factors, emerging windows, and next-72-hour staging.

## Scoring

Scoring is deterministic and normalized. Exact weights require fixtures and editorial review, but the initial formula must include:

- Evidence confidence and birth-time availability.
- Orb/exactness and whether the factor is applying, exact, or separating.
- Transit duration and planet speed.
- Natal target significance, including angles only when valid.
- Repetition/pass stage.
- Convergence with other independent signals.
- Moderating or contradictory conditions.
- Approved user priority domains.

Context may add a bounded relevance boost but never increase evidence confidence or intensity. Scores must not be described to users as probabilities of external events.

## Temporal classification

| State       | Deterministic basis                                                          | Narrative use                                |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Recent      | Exactness or ingress occurred inside the reviewed lookback and is separating | What has just shifted or is being integrated |
| Emerging    | Enters orb/window inside the forecast horizon                                | What is beginning to gather                  |
| Building    | Applying and not yet at the exact threshold                                  | What deserves preparation                    |
| Exact       | Inside the configured exactness tolerance                                    | What is most concentrated now                |
| Separating  | Past exactness but inside the active window                                  | What is releasing or clarifying              |
| Integrating | Long-duration factor recently exact/separating                               | What continues to settle into practice       |
| Recurring   | Linked repeated-pass series                                                  | What returns for review or completion        |

## Convergence and contradiction

- Signals converge only when they share a reviewed theme/domain mapping and derive from independent evidence.
- Repeated references to the same aspect do not count as independent convergence.
- A theme cluster lists supporting, moderating, and contradictory signals separately.
- The narrative must explain genuine contradiction instead of resolving it into false certainty.
- The BLUF must represent the strongest contradiction when it materially changes practical application.

## Practical applications

Applications are specific to the rule, domain, timing, and state. They are not interchangeable self-care filler.

Examples of rule-specific distinctions:

- An applying Mercury–Saturn contact may prioritize document review, sequencing, precise language, and allowing extra response time.
- A Venus ingress affecting the natal second house may prioritize value alignment, resource choices, and the terms of reciprocity.
- A separating Mars contact may emphasize debriefing conflict, repairing execution gaps, and releasing unnecessary urgency.
- A void-of-course Moon period, under the selected reviewed definition, may emphasize completion and review rather than launching a time-sensitive sequence.

Applications do not promise employment, money, relationship, legal, medical, or recovery outcomes.

## User context

- Important meeting: elevate communication/timing applications supported by relevant signals.
- Contract negotiation: elevate document, reciprocity, timing, and resource factors; do not provide legal advice.
- Financial decision: elevate resource/value themes; do not recommend transactions or predict returns.
- Relationship conversation: elevate communication/relational dynamics without predicting another person's behavior.
- Recovery: adults-only reviewed themes; no causation, diagnosis, treatment, medication, shame, relapse prediction, or free-text narrative.
- Crisis signals leave the astrology flow and display reviewed region-neutral emergency guidance.

## Unsupported claims

Rules and narrative must reject:

- Invented placements, houses, aspects, or exact times.
- Assertions that a specific event happened when the user did not provide it.
- Deterministic external outcomes or inevitability.
- Medical, legal, financial, mental-health, or substance-use diagnosis/prediction.
- House/angle claims for unknown birth time.
- Current-angle claims without an explicit validated observation location.
- Annual-technique claims before a separately reviewed method ships.
- Generic prose used to fill a section with insufficient evidence.
