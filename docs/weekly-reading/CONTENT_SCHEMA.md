# Weekly reading content schema

Version: `weekly-content-v1`.

The reading begins with Bottom Line Up Front and then presents the differentiating day-by-day map. It contains a localized headline/week range/method, a 500–650-word target BLUF, three to five day-tagged priorities, forward look, exactly seven evidence-linked day records, at least three thematic sections, practical applications, three to six journal questions, and limitations.

Zod rejects unknown fields, invalid dates/locales, missing days, unknown evidence, missing sections, and unknown-time house evidence. Every material day and section cites immutable evidence. Evidence IDs, rule IDs, timestamps, and degrees are never translated.

Use confident but non-deterministic reflective language. Do not diagnose, prescribe, guarantee, predict events, infer another person's private state, or provide medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice. Unsupported specificity is omitted rather than filled with generic copy.
