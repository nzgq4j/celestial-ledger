# Weekly reading content schema

Current version: `weekly-content-v5` (`weekly-content-v4` remains readable so existing private links do not break).

The reading begins with Bottom Line Up Front, then presents a seven-day relative-emphasis plot, a linked timeline, and readable daily guidance sections. It contains a localized headline/date range/method, a 500–650-word target BLUF, three to five day-tagged priorities, a forward look, exactly seven evidence-linked day records, two or three practical actions and one watch-for pattern per day, at least three thematic sections, three to six journal questions, and limitations.

Zod rejects unknown fields, invalid dates/locales, missing days, unknown evidence, missing sections, and unknown-time house evidence. Every material day and section retains immutable evidence bindings, but IDs and system terminology never appear in reader-facing prose. The visible evidence disclosure uses concise chart-factor labels; raw IDs remain internal.

Generated copy targets roughly 75% interpretation and practical guidance. Technical description is subordinate and may name only the chart factors needed to explain the guidance. Validation rejects server/system language, parenthetical evidence citations, and raw evidence identifiers.

Use confident but non-deterministic reflective language. Do not diagnose, prescribe, guarantee, predict events, infer another person's private state, or provide medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice. Unsupported specificity is omitted rather than filled with generic copy.
